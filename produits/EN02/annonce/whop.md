Physics Formula Sheet (College & Pre-Med)

# Accroche
College and MCAT physics on one page, with the medical applications.

# Description
What you get: a one-page physics formula sheet as four PDFs (A4, US Letter, grayscale for black-and-white printing, and a 3-page landscape version for tablets), plus an Anki deck of 25 flashcards.

What's on the page:
- 13 topic sections: units, kinematics, forces, energy, momentum, circular motion, fluids, waves, optics, electricity and magnetism, circuits, thermodynamics, nuclear
- Fluids and circulation: hydrostatic pressure, Bernoulli, Poiseuille, Laplace
- Optics sign table, thin lens, diopters and vision correction
- High-yield medical physics table: MAP, valve gradient, ultrasound and Doppler, inspired O₂, imaging effective doses, radiation weighting factors
- 14 worked examples with numbers
- 9 clinical notes and 12 exam traps
- Constants box (CODATA 2022 values, rounded) and unit conversions (atm, kPa, mmHg, cmH₂O, cal, kcal, eV, K)

For college physics students, pre-meds and MCAT candidates reviewing physics, and first-year medical students who need the physics behind circulation, breathing and imaging.

Written with AI assistance; content checked and approved by the seller.

A study aid. It complements your courses and reference books; it doesn't replace them.
