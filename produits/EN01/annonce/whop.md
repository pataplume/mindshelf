# Titre
Dosage Calculation & IV Drip Rates (Nursing Formula Sheet)

# Accroche
Nursing medication math on one page, with 9 worked examples and 9 common traps.

# Description
What you get: a one-page dosage calculation sheet as four PDFs (A4, US Letter, grayscale for black-and-white printing, and a 3-page landscape version for tablets), plus an Anki deck of 24 flashcards.

What's on the page:
- 10 sections, from core methods and unit conversions to safe notation, medication rights and complete orders
- Desired over have (D/H × Q), ratio–proportion and dimensional analysis side by side
- IV rates: mL/h, gtt/min with drop factors, the 60-drop shortcut, end time
- Weight-based infusions: mcg/kg/min ↔ mL/h, units/kg/h → mL/h
- Safe-range check (per dose vs per day), BSA (Mosteller), reconstitution, % and ratio strength
- The ×1,000 ladder and exact NIST household values next to label values
- Rounding to pump steps and syringe marks; a never-write/write table of 9 error-prone notations
- 9 worked examples with every step shown, 9 common traps, 2 clinical notes

All examples use fictional drugs (Drug A to F) with practice values, not dosing guidance.

For nursing students preparing for dosage calculation exams, and NCLEX candidates reviewing medication math.
