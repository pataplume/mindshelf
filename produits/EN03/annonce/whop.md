# Description
What you get: a one-page chemistry formula sheet as four PDFs (A4, US Letter, grayscale for black-and-white printing, and a 3-page landscape version for tablets), plus an Anki deck of 25 flashcards.

What's on the page:
- 14 sections: units and concentration, atoms and periodic trends, bonding and geometry, gases, thermochemistry, kinetics and enzymes, equilibrium and solubility, acids, bases and buffers, solutions and colligative properties, electrochemistry, organic essentials, spectroscopy, high-yield medical chemistry, constants and traps
- Core formulas with 14 short worked examples
- 6 tables: VSEPR geometry, enzyme inhibitors, acid–base disorders, SN1 vs SN2, IR bands, ¹H NMR shifts
- High-yield medical chemistry: anion gap, serum osmolality, O₂ content and drug half-life, with reference intervals for anion gap and osmolality
- 4 clinical notes: bicarbonate buffer, membrane (Nernst) potential, pulse oximetry, alveolar gas equation
- Key constants and 6 common exam traps

For college and pre-med students reviewing general and organic chemistry, MCAT candidates, and first-year medical students.

Written with AI assistance; content checked and approved by the seller.
